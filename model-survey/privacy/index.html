Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026. Applies to the Model Survey app for iPad.

Data on Your Device

Model Survey stores your surveys, plans, sketches, readings, notes, and room scans in its on-device app storage. The app does not upload your surveys to developer-operated servers and makes no network connections of its own. It has no user accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

Camera and LiDAR

Camera access is requested only when you choose to photograph a plan or scan a room. A room scan is processed on your iPad using Apple's room-scanning framework. The app keeps the resulting floor plan and 3D model with the survey; it does not keep or send the camera video.

Photos and Files

The app receives only the photos you select in the system photo picker and the files you choose in the system file picker.

Exports, Files Providers, and Backups

When you export or share a figure, workbook, plan, 3D model, or survey file, you choose the destination using the system sharing interface. That destination may receive the content you share. Files providers, iCloud, device backups, email services, and other apps operate under their own policies and your device settings.

Retention and Deletion

Surveys remain until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting a survey removes its file and any scan model stored with it. Previously exported or shared files and backup copies may remain with their destinations; manage those separately. Keep your own backups of important work.

Apple Services

Apple processes purchases and may process diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies. If you share diagnostics with developers, Apple may make that information available to the developer. The app does not include a separate developer analytics service.

Support Messages

If you email info@apsdrone.com, the developer receives the email address, message, and any attachments you send. These are used to respond to and investigate your request. Do not send sensitive or confidential project data. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the same address, subject to records that must be retained for legal or security reasons. Email providers process messages under their own policies.

These Web Pages

The two Model Survey pages, Support and Privacy, contain no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or uploads. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the visitor's IP address and ordinary request details for security purposes and handles that information under its own privacy statement. This is separate from the app's on-device storage.

These pages share the apsdrone.com domain with a separate business website. That website, including the page shown for an address that does not exist, uses analytics and has its own privacy notice. It is not part of Model Survey, and the app never opens it.

Questions and Changes

Contact info@apsdrone.com for privacy questions. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
