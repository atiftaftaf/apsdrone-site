Model Survey Support

Floor elevation surveys on iPad. View on the App Store

Contact Support

Email info@apsdrone.com with your app version, iPad model, iPadOS version, and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords, financial information, or confidential project files. If a sample is needed, use a survey you have permission to share.

Starting a Survey

New Survey opens a blank page where you sketch the floor outline with a finger or Apple Pencil. From a Plan starts from a photo, a PDF or image file, a blank grid, or a LiDAR room scan. Sample Project opens a finished example to look around.

Set the scale by marking a known distance on the plan, then tap where each reading was taken and type its value. Surveys save as you work.

Maps and Profiles

The Elevation, Slope, and Curvature views are drawn from your readings. Draw a profile line to see a chart and values along it. The panel lists every reading and the survey settings.

LiDAR Room Scan

Scanning needs an iPad with a LiDAR scanner and asks for camera access. Move slowly along the walls. The scan produces a floor plan with wall lengths, doors, and windows, and a 3D model. Use Edit Plan to move corners, set wall lengths, or add and remove walls, doors, and windows when the scan is not right. Scanned dimensions are estimates; check important lengths with a tape.

Files and Exports

Surveys are stored on your iPad as .msurvey files, visible in the Files app under Model Survey. Keep your own copies of important work before removing the app or changing devices.

Export creates a figure (PNG or PDF), the readings as an Excel workbook, the floor plan (PDF or PNG), a 3D model of a scan (USDZ), or the survey file itself.

Accuracy

Results depend on your readings, your instrument, and the scale you set. Model Survey is a recording and drawing tool, not certified surveying or engineering software, and it does not judge whether a floor or foundation is acceptable. Have a qualified professional interpret the results.
