Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026. Applies to the Model Notes app for iPad and iPhone.

Data on Your Device

Model Notes stores imported models, annotations, measurements, notes, and selected photos in its on-device app storage. The app does not upload your projects to developer-operated servers. It has no user accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

Permissions

The app accesses model files you choose using the system file picker. It imports photos you select using the system photo picker. Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a note photo. You can change camera permission in device Settings. Photos added to notes are stored with the local project.

Exports, Files Providers, and Backups

When you export or share a project, report, or video, you choose the destination using the system sharing interface. That destination may receive the content you share. Files providers, iCloud, device backups, email services, and other apps operate under their own policies and your device settings.

Retention and Deletion

Saved projects remain until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting a model removes its local project data. Clear Cache & Exports removes temporary export copies, not saved projects. Previously shared files and backup copies may remain with their destinations; manage those separately. Keep your own backups of important work.

Apple Services and Beta Testing

Apple processes purchases and may process diagnostics and TestFlight feedback according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies. If you send TestFlight feedback or diagnostics, Apple may make that information available to the developer. The app does not include a separate developer analytics service.

Support Messages

If you email info@apsdrone.com, the developer receives the email address, message, and any attachments you send. These are used to respond to and investigate your request. Do not send sensitive or confidential project data. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the same address, subject to records that must be retained for legal or security reasons. Email providers process messages under their own policies.

These Web Pages

The two Model Notes pages, Support and Privacy, contain no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or model uploads. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the visitor's IP address and ordinary request details for security purposes and handles that information under its own privacy statement. This is separate from the app's on-device project storage.

These pages share the apsdrone.com domain with a separate business website. That website, including the page shown for an address that does not exist, uses analytics and has its own privacy notice. It is not part of Model Notes, and the app never opens it.

Questions and Changes

Contact info@apsdrone.com for privacy questions. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date and, when appropriate, in the app.
